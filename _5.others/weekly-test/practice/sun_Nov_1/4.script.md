
So, the problem: given a string like I V or X I I, return its number value. I V is 4, X I I is 12.

My method is pair-checking with lookahead. Here is my thinking. Some pairs change the value. I V is not 1 plus 5 — it is 4. So I cannot just add each letter one by one. I have to look one letter ahead. If the two letters make a special pair, I take the pair value and jump over both. If not, I take the single letter value and move on.

So I keep a small dictionary for single values. Then I walk through the string with an index. At each step I check the current letter and the next one. Three special cases: I before V or X, X before L or C, C before D or M. Special pair — add the value, jump 2. Normal letter — add the value, go 1. At the end, total is the answer.

One example: M C M X C I V. M is 1000, C M is 900, X C is 90, I V is 4. Total is 1994.

Time is O of n. Space is O of 1. Thank you.
